Privacy Policy

Book Ratings, by Obscura Logic — effective September 28, 2026

Overview

Book Ratings ("the App") is developed by Obscura Logic. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can request that your data be deleted. The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address, first name, and last name. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed using PBKDF2 (HMAC-SHA256) with a unique random salt before it reaches our database, and it cannot be recovered by us.

Content you create. Content ratings you submit for books (violence, language, sexual content, substance use, horror, self-harm, religion, and abuse, each on a 0–5 scale), an overall rating, and any free-text review comments you write. Your reading status and progress for books (want to read / currently reading / read / did not finish, and page or percent progress), and any custom shelves you create.

Social information. If you use the friends feature, we store friend requests and friendships you create, and we show connected friends limited information about your reading activity (such as books you've rated or marked as read) and your name.

Ratings and reviews you submit are visible to other users as part of the App's community rating and review features (for example, aggregated content ratings and public review text shown alongside a book).

Camera. The App requests camera access solely to scan a book's ISBN barcode when you use the scan feature. The camera image itself is never stored or transmitted — only the decoded barcode number is used to look up the book.

Technical information. Our servers may log standard technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security, abuse prevention, and operational troubleshooting. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling.

Crash and diagnostic data. The App uses Sentry to automatically capture crash reports and error logs so we can find and fix bugs. This can include your device model and OS version, app version, in-app diagnostic logs, and (for crashes) a snapshot of what was on screen in the moments before the crash, with text and inputs masked. If you submit feedback through the App's "Send feedback" option, the message you write (and, optionally, your name or email if you choose to include them) is also sent to Sentry.

Advertising. The App shows a small number of clearly labelled "Sponsored" ads, delivered by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only, meaning ads are not targeted based on your interests or browsing history, and we limit ads to general-audience content. To serve, measure, frequency-cap, and prevent fraud on these ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and whether you viewed or tapped an ad. We do not share your account information, email, name, ratings, reviews, or reading activity with Google or any advertiser.

Book purchase links. Book pages may include a "Buy on Bookshop.org" link. These are affiliate links: if you buy a book after following one, Obscura Logic may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Following the link simply opens Bookshop.org; we do not send Bookshop.org any of your account information, and any purchase you make there is governed by Bookshop.org's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To store and display your ratings, reviews, and reading activity, and to compute community-aggregated content ratings for books.
  • To show ads that help fund the App, and to earn affiliate commissions on book purchases made through our links.
  • To generate an AI-estimated content rating for books that don't yet have enough community ratings (see below).
  • To provide social features such as friends and shared reading activity, where you choose to use them.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App.

Third parties we share data with

Book data providers (isbndb and Google Books). When you search for a book, we may query the isbndb API, and if needed the Google Books API, to look up book metadata (title, author, cover, etc.). These requests are made by our servers, and only the search terms you enter are sent — no account or personal information is shared with these providers.

AI content-rating estimates (Anthropic). For books without enough community ratings yet, the App may ask Anthropic's Claude API to generate an estimated content rating. Only the book's own metadata (such as title and author) is sent for this purpose — your account information, identity, or personal data is never included in these requests.

Crash and diagnostic reporting (Sentry). We use Sentry to receive the crash, diagnostic, and feedback data described above so we can diagnose and fix problems with the App. Sentry processes this data on our behalf under its own security and data-processing terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob). Google collects the device and ad-interaction information described above directly through its SDK in the App, and processes it under its own terms. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Bookshop.org. When you follow a purchase link, you leave the App for Bookshop.org. Bookshop.org reports affiliate sales to us so commissions can be paid; we do not receive your name, contact details, or payment information from them.

Hosting infrastructure. The App's backend and database run on infrastructure operated by Obscura Logic. We do not share your data with any other third party beyond those named in this policy, and we never sell personal data.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and content for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by emailing privacy@obscuralogic.com from the email address on your account. We will delete your personal data completely — not merely deactivate it — except where a short retention period is required to comply with the law or resolve disputes. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), and authentication uses short-lived tokens that are rotated on use. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices

You can edit your content preferences, ratings, and reviews at any time within the App. You may delete individual ratings, reviews, shelves, or friend connections from within the App. To delete your account entirely, contact us as described above.

Ads. If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA or UK), the App asks for your choice before showing ads, and you can change it later under Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you decline, you may still see a limited form of non-personalized ads. On Android, you can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App.
